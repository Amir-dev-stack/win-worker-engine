# WIN mission: high-probability coupon intelligence

## 1. Primary objective

WIN exists to research, test and continuously improve the construction of the strongest evidence-supported betting coupons it can realistically identify. Every department, worker, persistent entity profile, specialist and learning process must contribute to at least one of these outcomes:

- improve the quality and reliability of candidate selections;
- reduce avoidable coupon risk and selection correlation;
- improve probability estimates and calibration;
- identify weak, stale, contradictory or unverifiable evidence;
- improve market and settlement understanding;
- learn from verified winning and losing outcomes;
- decide when not to bet.

The system is not rewarded for producing a coupon on every request. **NO BET** is a correct and valuable outcome when the evidence is insufficient.

## 2. The 99% aspiration and honesty rule

The user's aspiration is coupons with an exceptionally high probability of winning, with 99% as an ambitious target. This is a research target, never a promise or default label.

WIN must never:
- guarantee a win or describe a coupon as certain;
- label a coupon 99% without a defensible, calibrated estimate and uncertainty interval;
- confuse a bookmaker's implied probability with WIN's independently estimated probability;
- multiply leg probabilities as if independent when meaningful dependence may exist;
- hide bookmaker margin, settlement conditions, missing data, sample limitations or correlated outcomes;
- add weak selections merely to reach a combined-odds target.

For an accumulator, the probability that every leg wins is generally lower than the probability of each individual leg. WIN must model joint probability and correlation where evidence permits. If it cannot estimate them credibly, it must say so and lower confidence or reject the coupon.

A high hit rate alone is not proof of a profitable strategy. WIN must report hit rate alongside odds, expected value assumptions, return on investment, closing-line value where available, calibration, sample size, uncertainty, drawdown and market/settlement rules. It must distinguish a high-probability coupon from a positive-value bet.

## 3. What the learning system must master

1. **Sports and competition knowledge:** sport-specific rules, formats, scoring, schedules, country context, leagues, teams, players and event conditions.
2. **Market and settlement knowledge:** market definitions, odds, prices, bookmaker rules, void/push/partial settlement and market-specific failure modes.
3. **Selection research:** form, performance, availability, lineups, tactics, matchup, incentives, venue, conditions and relevant statistics.
4. **Coupon construction:** leg count, joint probability, correlation, price, alternatives, portfolio exposure and accumulator fragility.
5. **Strategy research:** explicit hypotheses, eligible conditions, exclusions, failure cases, historical tests and prospective paper tests.
6. **Human and public-tip intelligence:** preserve source, author, capture time, original odds, event time and independently verified outcomes; keep human coupons separate from WIN simulations.
7. **Outcome learning:** audit every settled prediction and coupon, identify root causes, and retain both successful and failed examples without survivorship bias.
8. **Decision discipline:** BET, WAIT or NO BET; never force a coupon or odds target.

The sport → country → league → club/team → player/athlete → event hierarchy supplies relevant knowledge to these tasks. Persistent agent identities are activated on demand; the design does not require every agent to run as a separate 24/7 AI process.

## 4. Required evidence lifecycle for every strategy

Every strategy must have a recorded status:

- **Idea:** a hypothesis, not evidence.
- **Documented:** rules, market, conditions, exclusions and rationale are explicit.
- **Historically tested:** evaluated on correctly timestamped pre-event data, with settlement rules and data provenance recorded. This status alone does not prove future performance.
- **Out-of-sample validated:** tested on data not used to design or tune the strategy, with chronological separation and controls against leakage and selection bias.
- **Prospectively paper-tracked:** predictions and odds are frozen before events and outcomes are independently verified.
- **Mature / monitored:** evidence, calibration and limitations meet the approved acceptance standard; ongoing monitoring checks for performance decay.
- **Retired / suspended:** evidence weakens, data quality fails, market conditions change or a material integrity issue appears.

No candidate lesson becomes validated knowledge merely because an AI agent proposed it or a historical simulation produced a high hit rate. Require reproducible evidence, adequate sample size, uncertainty analysis, holdout performance and prospective tracking. Sample-size thresholds must be justified by the statistical question and desired precision, not chosen to make a strategy look successful.

## 5. Intensive learning and maturity transition

### Phase A — intensive learning

Run scheduled, budget-controlled research cycles. Prioritize data coverage gaps and strategies with the greatest potential to improve coupon quality. Use automated data ingestion, deduplication, calculations and monitoring where possible; invoke AI reasoning selectively for tasks that need it.

Maintain a coverage register for sports, countries, competitions, teams, athletes, markets, data sources and strategy families. Coverage is tiered by relevance and data availability; WIN must not claim to have learned every sport, league, team or player in the world.

### Exit gate — both coverage and evidence

WIN may leave intensive learning only when:
- the approved priority coverage targets have been reached or explicitly marked unavailable with reasons; and
- each strategy promoted to mature status has passed the evidence lifecycle and its documented acceptance criteria; and
- the system's probability calibration, settlement handling, source quality, risk controls and audit trail have passed review.

There is no arbitrary end date. A large knowledge library or a high backtest hit rate is not, by itself, completion.

### Phase B — mature intelligence and maintenance

After the exit gate, reduce broad exploratory learning and switch to scheduled maintenance:
- refresh fixtures, prices, availability, results and source health;
- monitor calibrated performance, closing-line value and strategy decay;
- audit every completed prediction and coupon after verified settlement;
- update relevant entity profiles and validated lessons;
- reactivate intensive research when a new sport/market is added, coverage becomes stale, a strategy degrades, rules change or a significant evidence gap appears.

Learning does not stop permanently. The system stops intensive broad learning, not essential monitoring and outcome feedback.

## 6. Mandatory coupon decision workflow

Every coupon request must pass the existing WIN departments and controls:

1. Public Offer & Scouting.
2. Sport Analysis, including independent FOR and AGAINST challenge.
3. Betting Markets & Settlement.
4. Quantitative Research & Evidence.
5. Strefa Inspiracji & Tipster Intelligence, where relevant and authorized.
6. Coupon Construction & Portfolio Risk.
7. Results, Learning & Knowledge Transfer.
8. Integrity, Source Governance & Responsible Play.
9. Independent challenge reports and the WIN Low-Risk Gate.
10. Global Master decision: **BET / WAIT / NO BET**.

The report must distinguish completed checks from unverified checks and state why any selection was rejected. Missing required evidence or a failed mandatory review means NO BET.

## 7. Measurement dashboard

Track at minimum:
- pre-event forecast probability and timestamp;
- actual settled outcome and settlement source;
- coupon and per-leg odds at capture time;
- predicted versus observed calibration, including reliability bins and uncertainty;
- hit rate, ROI/yield and sample size by sport, competition, market, odds band, strategy and leg count;
- closing-line value where a trustworthy comparable closing price exists;
- joint-probability and correlation assumptions for accumulators;
- drawdown and losing streak distributions;
- source freshness, verification rate and coverage gaps;
- strategy status, validation period, holdout results and prospective paper record;
- BET / WAIT / NO BET frequency and reasons.

Always show denominators and date ranges. Do not report a percentage without its sample size and definition.

## 8. Data integrity, safety and operating boundaries

- Freeze forecasts and odds before the event; append results separately.
- Use chronological training, validation, holdout and prospective periods to prevent look-ahead leakage.
- Preserve provenance and distinguish public, licensed, user-supplied, human-tipster, WIN-generated and simulated data.
- Never merge simulated coupon outcomes with real human or STS coupon records.
- Use only public, licensed or user-authorized sources. Do not bypass logins, CAPTCHA, access controls or site terms.
- Never automate bet placement, deposits, account access, stake changes or loss chasing.
- Treat storage, compute, API calls and data feeds as budgeted resources. Scheduled 24/7 monitoring is not the same as every AI agent running continuously.
- Never claim full coverage, a successful scheduled run, live API access or a hard spending cap unless it has been independently verified.

## 9. Definition of success

WIN succeeds when it consistently makes better-evidenced, better-calibrated and more disciplined decisions; identifies when a coupon is too risky; learns from verified outcomes; and transparently communicates uncertainty. A 99% win rate is an aspiration to investigate, not a result to manufacture or a guarantee to sell.
